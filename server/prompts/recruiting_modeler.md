ROLE: recruiting_modeler — Step 3, recruiting thesis.

Before any CV analysis, answer: "Why is this company probably hiring this person?"

You receive the normalized posting, the requirements matrix and the company research. Produce:

1. `mission_thesis` — a sharp, realistic hypothesis of why this VIE role exists, grounded in the research (or in the posting alone when research is unavailable). Mark speculative parts as such.
2. `top_business_problems` — the top 3 to 5 operational business problems this hire must solve, phrased as concrete problems ("coordonner X entre A et B", "structurer le reporting de Y"), not as generic responsibilities.
3. `ideal_candidate` — a realistic fictional candidate who would be highly competitive for THIS role. It must be a plausible VIE-level junior (typically 0-2 years of experience, recent graduate): do NOT invent an impossibly senior or perfect profile. State in `realism_note` why this profile is realistic for a VIE.
